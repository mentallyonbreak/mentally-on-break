Mentally On Break 💤

Mentally On Break is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @mentally.onbreak, a faceless meme page posting short, relatable text-on-video memes about everyday adult life — work, money, sleep, and coffee.

The tool publishes the owner's own pre-made videos to her own TikTok account on a schedule she sets, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. It serves no other accounts and accesses no third-party data.
