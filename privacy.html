Privacy Policy — Mentally On Break (TikTok automation app)

Last updated: October 17, 2026

"Mentally On Break" is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the TikTok account @mentally.onbreak. It exists for one purpose: to publish the account owner's own pre-made videos to her own TikTok account on a schedule she sets, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

What data the app accesses

  • TikTok authorization tokens (access token and refresh token) granted by the account owner through TikTok's official OAuth login, for the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish.
  • Basic account information returned by TikTok (such as the account's display name and username) to confirm the correct account is connected.
  • The video files, captions, and posting schedule the owner provides for publication.

How the data is used

Data is used only to (1) confirm the connected TikTok account, (2) upload and publish the owner's own videos to her own account, and (3) keep the authorization valid by refreshing the access token. The app does not read other users' data, does not collect analytics about third parties, does not sell or rent data, and does not use data for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored in encrypted credential storage accessible only to the app operator's automation environment. Video files are stored on the operator's own systems until published.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are retained only while the owner uses the app. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Manage apps and permissions), which immediately invalidates the tokens. On request, all stored tokens and unpublished video files will be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the TikTok account @mentally.onbreak.
